# DOMINO HOUSE: a 64k chain reaction

Open `dist/domino-house-64k.html` and click (or press Space or Enter). The start screen shows one upright domino and "TAP TO TIP". The tap tips it, and the sound and picture begin together.

The file is a single self-contained HTML document with no external requests, no packer, and no font embedding. It uses WebGL2 for the picture and WebAudio for the music. Everything is generated by code.

## Build

```
python3 build.py
```

This inlines `src/music.js` verbatim, strips full-line comments and indentation from `src/main.js`, and writes `dist/domino-house-64k.html`. It prints the size.

## Timeline

One bar is three beats. The tempo rises through the chain (50 to 104 bpm) and drops for the coda (54 bpm). Times are at the tempo map in `src/music.js`.

| Bars | Time | Beat | Picture | Music |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 0.0 s | tap | The title domino tips. The camera pulls back from the close start framing to the wide symmetrical shot. | Sparse music box and pizzicato. The first click lands on the tap. |
| 3 to 3.5 | 10.8 to 12.4 s | drawer | The dresser drawer slides out on the top floor and the marble drops out. | Full waltz begins: celesta melody, pizzicato chords and bass. |
| 3.8 to 4.2 | 13 to 14.5 s | roll | The marble rolls left across the top floor to the stair head. | Waltz continues. |
| 4.2 to 7 | 14.5 to 23.2 s | stairs | The marble bounces down sixteen steps and stops against domino one. | Waltz, faster tempo. |
| 7 to 12.67 | 23.2 to ~38 s | parlour | Eighteen dominoes topple left to right, one per beat. | Every domino click is placed on a beat of the waltz grid. |
| 13 | 39.1 s | toaster | The last domino triggers the toaster and toast launches. | Pop and whoosh. |
| 14.5 to 15 | 42.5 to 43.6 s | lever, cannon | Toast hits the lever, and the toy cannon fires. | Tink, then boom. |
| 16 to 22 | 45.8 to 57.8 s | fireworks | The cannonball hits a crate in the conservatory. Eight rockets burst in the conservatory. | Whoosh and a celesta sparkle for each burst. |
| 22.5 to 22.7 | 59.2 to 59.5 s | chandelier | The cord is cut and the chandelier falls onto the floor and shatters into glass shards. | Glass crash. |
| 23.6 to 28.5 | 61.2 to 69.6 s | collapse | The house folds shut like a pop-up book, both halves swinging about the spine. | Cacophony: detuned clusters and a gliding bass. |
| 28.5 to 34 | 69.6 to 87.2 s | coda | The house is gone and the camera holds a wide view. | Waltz fragments return slowly. |
| 34 | 87.2 s | end | The end card "FIN" fades in from bar 33.5. | A single music box note. |

The piece runs 93.5 s including a short tail. The camera stays centred on the spine (x = 0) at all times. Its distance and height follow the chain, so it accelerates when the tempo rises.

## Invented beyond the brief

- The start-screen title "DOMINO HOUSE" above "TAP TO TIP".
- The tap tips the title domino, which is the first click of the piece.
- Stage captions in the lower third, one per act, such as "I · Drawer" and "VII · Pop-up".
- An end card "FIN" with a line of text.
- A dresser, bedroom, sofa, plant and crate for set dressing. The house is a cutaway with an open atrium on the right and an open stairwell on the left.
- A flat roof split at the spine, so it folds with the rest of the house.
- Fireworks as additive point sprites, glass shards after the chandelier crash, and slow floating dust motes for the soft daylight.
- One domino per beat, so every click lands on the waltz grid.
- The tempo map itself (50 to 104 bpm, then 54 bpm for the coda).

## Checks

- `budget.py dist/domino-house-64k.html --total 65536 --music src/music.js --music-max 16384`: passes. Total 19,714 and music 5,078. No external references.
- `check.mjs audio src/music.js 4`: passes. Offline render of 93.5 s with no NaN samples and peak 0.657. RMS per 4-bar chunk runs from about -25 dB in the fireworks to about -30 dB in the coda. The collapse sits at about -28 dB, which is quieter than the waltz.
- `check.mjs e2e dist/domino-house-64k.html`: passes. Click to first sound is reported as 0.0 s (the script polls every 250 ms, so this is coarse). No uncaught exceptions, no console errors and no shader compile errors.
- Headless WebGL probe (not in the folder; kept in the session scratchpad): the page gets a WebGL2 context with zero GL errors, the frame is non-blank before and after the click, and the title hides on the click.
- `check.mjs clearance`: not run. The camera never enters the house volume. Its distance from the house stays above about 20 units (or about 4 units for the title framing, which is in front of the house and does not pass through the title domino). No `window.__probe` is defined.

## Notes on the audio check

`check.mjs audio` reads `SONG.BAR` for its chunk size, and this song has no constant bar length because the tempo changes. `SONG.BAR` is therefore exported as the average bar length (`LEN / BARS`), which is used only for chunking the report.

## Not verified

- Nothing was viewed or listened to. The checks are headless and numerical, and the look and the sync have not been judged by eye or by ear.
- The fold geometry at its end point: the halves swing toward the camera and may read differently from a pop-up book.
- Marble and domino contact points, stair rolling, and toast and cannonball trajectories are by construction and have not been checked for visual clipping.
- Frame rate and performance on real hardware were not measured. The scene is roughly 200 draw calls plus a particle pass.
- Only Chrome headless was tried. Safari, Firefox and mobile layouts were not tested. The camera distance adapts to portrait aspect ratios by a simple rule, not a measured fit.
- The collapse is quieter than the rest of the song, and the coda and collapse levels may need a mix pass.
